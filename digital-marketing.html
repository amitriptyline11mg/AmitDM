What I'm Learning

Digital Marketing, Explored

Digital marketing is a wide discipline. Here's an honest overview of the areas I'm actively exploring — what each involves, why it matters, and how I'm practising it.

The Disciplines

Core Learning Areas

Each area below represents a real domain of digital marketing — not surface-level definitions, but practical skills that take time to develop.

🔍

Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

SEO is the practice of making your website more visible in search engine results pages (SERPs). It's not about tricking Google — it's about creating content people genuinely want to find and making it easy for search engines to understand.

I'm learning SEO by practising it directly on this site: writing content with proper heading structure, optimising meta titles and descriptions, building internal links between pages, and submitting the sitemap to Google Search Console.

On-page SEO Technical SEO Keyword Research Internal Linking Search Console Structured Data

Read: SEO for Beginners →

📈

Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is a free tool that shows you what's happening on your website — how many people visited, which pages they read, where they came from, and what actions they took.

I'm learning how to set up GA4, configure events and conversions, read the core reports, and make data-informed decisions. The goal is to understand not just the interface, but what the numbers actually mean.

Events Conversions Audiences Acquisition Reports Engagement Metrics Funnel Analysis

Read: GA4 vs GTM →

🏷️

Google Tag Manager

Google Tag Manager (GTM) is a tag management system that lets you add tracking code to your website without editing the code directly. For marketers, this is a game-changer — no more waiting for a developer to add a tracking pixel.

I'm learning the three core concepts of GTM — Tags (what fires), Triggers (when it fires), and Variables (dynamic values) — by implementing them on this site to track real user behaviour.

Tags Triggers Variables DataLayer Preview Mode Publishing

Read: What Is GTM? →

✍️

Content Marketing

Content marketing is the practice of creating useful, relevant content to attract and engage an audience — rather than interrupting them with ads. Done well, it builds trust, generates organic search traffic, and supports every other marketing channel.

This blog is my content marketing practice environment. I'm learning how to plan content around search intent, structure articles for readability, write compelling headlines, and build internal links that help both readers and search engines.

Blogging Content Strategy Headline Writing Content Structure Internal Linking

Read the blog →

🎯

Conversion Rate Optimisation

CRO is the practice of improving what percentage of your website visitors take a desired action — clicking a CTA, filling a form, signing up for a newsletter. It combines psychology, design, and data to make websites more effective.

I'm applying CRO principles directly to this site — designing CTAs with clear value propositions, creating landing pages with conversion intent, and learning to read analytics data to identify drop-off points.

CTA Design Landing Pages A/B Testing Principles Conversion Tracking Heatmaps

Read: How to Measure Conversions →

💰

Paid Search & Social Ads

Paid search (Google Ads) and paid social (Meta Ads, LinkedIn) are channels where you pay to show adverts to people based on what they search for or who they are. Unlike SEO, paid channels can generate traffic immediately — but stop the moment you stop paying.

This area is on my learning roadmap. I'm building the foundations first — understanding keywords, audiences, ad copy principles, and conversion tracking — before spending on campaigns.

Google Ads Meta Ads Keyword Bidding Audience Targeting Ad Copy

📧

Email Marketing

Despite being one of the oldest digital channels, email consistently outperforms other channels in terms of ROI. Building a list, creating good email content, and understanding deliverability are skills every digital marketer should have.

I'm exploring email marketing fundamentals: list segmentation, email copywriting principles, subject line optimisation, and connecting platforms like Mailchimp or Brevo to this website's newsletter form.

List Building Segmentation Email Copywriting Deliverability Automation

🗓️ Coming to the blog soon

📱

Social Media Marketing

Organic social media is about building an audience and creating content that people choose to engage with and share. Different platforms suit different content types and audiences — LinkedIn for professional content, Instagram for visual, Twitter/X for ideas and commentary.

I'm learning how organic social fits into a wider digital marketing strategy — as a distribution channel for content, a way to build an audience, and eventually to support paid campaigns.

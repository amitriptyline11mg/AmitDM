Hello, I'm Amit

I'm a QA professional making a deliberate shift into digital marketing. I've spent my career in software quality assurance — writing test cases, finding what's broken, thinking carefully about user flows, and making sure things actually work the way they're supposed to. Now I'm applying that same systematic, detail-driven mindset to a new challenge.

The switch made sense to me for a simple reason: QA and digital marketing are both fundamentally about understanding user behaviour and improving outcomes. In QA, you test to find failure points and fix them. In digital marketing, you measure to find improvement opportunities and act on them. The curiosity is the same — only the tools change. My background means I already think in data, already care about user experience, and already know how to test hypotheses rather than guess.

I'm learning by doing. This website is my practice environment — every page is built with real SEO principles, every CTA is designed with conversion intent, and once Google Tag Manager is installed, every click will be measurable. I write about what I learn as I learn it, which is both the most honest way to build a public record and the most effective way I've found to actually retain anything.

"QA taught me to never assume something works just because it looks like it should. Digital marketing is teaching me the same lesson about websites."

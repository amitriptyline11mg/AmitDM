💡 If you're just starting out:

The best thing I can say is: start with something real. Don't just take courses and read articles. Build a website, set up analytics, write some content, and see what happens. Learning by doing is orders of magnitude more effective than learning by watching. You can read SEO for Beginners and What Is Google Tag Manager? as starting points — then do something with what you learn.
